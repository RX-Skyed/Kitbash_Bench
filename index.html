Export

Overlapping parts and pieces are joined into a single closed shell, so nothing is left inside for the slicer to print as a void or a wall, and any hollow sealed completely inside is filled. Applies to every export: one file, each part, and sprues. Parts that only nearly touch stay separate: use Fill seams to join them.

Where parts nearly touch (seams, joints, pegs in holes), their surfaces are pushed out to overlap, so the slicer sees one solid with no slits. Nothing else changes. Applies to the one-file exports.

Air sealed inside the model, between parts or inside a part, is filled solid in the exported file so no uncured resin gets trapped. Your parts don't change: keep posing and moving them, it's worked out again each export. A cavity whose only way out is a crack narrower than the setting counts as sealed: resin can't drain through it. Pockets with a real drain hole, and specks under 1 mm³, are left alone. This also catches cavities with a narrow crack to the outside, which Merge alone leaves open.

Exports use the current pose, in millimetres.

Moving and the view

The view turns around the selected parts, or around whatever is under the cursor when nothing is selected. The wheel zooms toward the cursor. Double-click a spot to centre the view on it.

Picking

Each snap tool's settings sit in the bar under the menus while that tool is active. Ball or cup highlight spills past the rim? Lower the ball fit tolerance. Stops short of the rim on a rough sculpt? Raise it. Face highlight only covers part of a face? Raise the flat face tolerance; sculpted and decimated parts often need 0.05 mm or more.
